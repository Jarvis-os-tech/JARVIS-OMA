# JARVIS-OMA memory vault

Open [[JARVIS-OMA Memory/Memory Map]] to browse JARVIS's memory.

Open Graph view to see facts, profiles, documents, topics, and the continuous
conversation timeline. Shared-topic links are based on words in the saved data.

JARVIS synchronizes the `JARVIS-OMA Memory` folder automatically while running.
Keep your own notes outside that folder. If you edit a generated note, JARVIS
preserves it and reports a sync conflict instead of overwriting your work.
